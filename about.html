About Elevate School

Elevate School was founded in 2020 with a mission to empower underserved youth through education. Our vision is to create a world where every young person has access to skills that lead to meaningful employment. We value innovation, community, and excellence in all we do.

Our Core Values

Innovation

Community

We build strong networks to support our students.

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in teaching and learning.
